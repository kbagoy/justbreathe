Your Therapist

Meet JoJo Bonino, LMT

Clinician, Licensed Massage Therapist & Adventure Seeker

Credentials

Licensed Massage Therapist · License #26842

Former Respiratory Care Practitioner · 22+ Years Medical Experience

With over 20 years in respiratory care and nearly a decade in massage therapy, JoJo blends a deep clinical understanding of the body with intentional, evidence-informed touch to help her clients heal, breathe easier, and live better.

After decades working as a Respiratory Therapist, JoJo took a leap of faith inspired by a motivational speech and left an established career to become a Licensed Massage Therapist. Nearly 10 years and two countries later, she's honored to run her own mobile massage practice, bringing healing to individuals who are often desperate for pain relief.

JoJo's medical background is what sets her apart, allowing her to offer specialized care most massage practices simply can't.

In her experienced, welcoming hands, you'll rest deeply in the comfort of your own space while your body does what it does best: heal.
